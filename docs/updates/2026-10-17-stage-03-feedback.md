## Design implications and interpretation

The dollar-estimate condition cannot serve as the working baseline for the proposed star experiment: when all agents copy the estimate, claims total $8.57 million, the allocator pays every claim in full, and the observed outcomes have no variation for network position to explain. It remains useful as a comparison arm. This guidance does not alter historical baselines or their saved results.

The professor supports the repeated-probe design and wants enough repetitions to compare the stronger-message responses with ordinary response variability. Define the signed change in A's claim before execution, preserve unexpected increases, and keep the saved state and request-order provenance.

The second-model request concerns the percentage-estimate diagnostic. Methodological distinction: reproducing that arm alone checks its behavior on another model; claiming a replicated **format contrast** would also require a matched dollar-estimate arm. The exact second model and any additional comparison have not yet been selected or executed.

Actual supplied estimates in Stage 03 were dollar amounts in both estimate conditions; the manipulated factor was the requested output unit. Use that precise description when writing the standalone result.
